Pace Hero · prototype

Privacy policy

Effective November 10, 2026

In short

  • Your runs stay on your phone. Nothing is uploaded automatically.
  • A run's data is shared only when you choose to send it to me, and the email shows you what's attached first.
  • No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

Who this covers

Pace Hero is an early prototype of a running app, made by Eric Blattberg and shared through Apple's TestFlight with a small group of people I know. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

What the app uses, and why

  • Location. GPS measures your running pace. The app uses it on the workout screens, during a workout (including with your screen locked) and on the GPS check screen, and not otherwise.
  • Motion & Fitness. The step counter and motion sensors help the app tell how you're carrying your phone, for a steadier pace. They're used only while the app is measuring your pace.
  • Your settings, such as your easy pace and sound choices, so the app remembers them.

You can turn location and motion access off any time in Settings → Pace Hero. The app can't measure your pace without location.

What's saved on your phone

Each workout is saved on your phone, in the app's own storage, so you can see it again in the History tab. A saved run includes:

  • your GPS route: positions and times, which show where and when you ran
  • your pace, speed and distance, and which cues and announcements played
  • motion readings, if a testing option for recording them is on
  • the app's settings, your phone's model, iOS version and battery level

None of this leaves your phone unless you send or export it.

When you send a run

The summary screen has a "Send run to Eric" button. It opens an email (or your phone's share sheet) with that run's data attached, addressed to me. You see it before anything is sent, can add a note, and can cancel. The email travels through your own email or messaging service, under its privacy terms.

When I receive a run, I use it only to understand how the app performed and to improve it. I may analyze it with software tools, including AI-assisted tools. I keep it private, don't share it with anyone else, and never sell it. If you'd like a run you sent deleted, email me and I will delete it.

TestFlight

Apple's TestFlight may share crash reports, the feedback and screenshots you choose to send, and basic device information with me. Apple handles that under its own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

  • To delete one run, swipe left on it in the History tab.
  • Deleting the app removes every run and setting it saved.
  • To delete runs you've sent me, email me.

Children

Pace Hero is a prototype meant for adults. It isn't intended for children.

Changes

If what the app does with your information changes, I'll update this page and its effective date.
